# Completed features

Finished feature work orders are archived here as `NN-name.md`, one per
feature, when `/complete` closes them out. Together they are the project's build
history: what was built, in what order, and why.

The spec in `docs/features/` or `docs/specs/` remains the contract. This is the
record of the work that satisfied it.

_Empty until you complete your first feature._
